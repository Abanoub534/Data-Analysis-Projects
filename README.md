# Data-Analyst-Projects

A collection of data analysis projects built from scratch, covering data preparation, statistical analysis, and business intelligence reporting.

## Projects

### [COVID-19 Global Analysis](covid-19/README.md)

Global analysis of the pandemic from January 1, 2020 to April 30, 2021.

Using SQL, Python, and Power BI, it covers cases, deaths, case fatality rates, vaccination progress, transmission dynamics, and data quality across 219 countries and 6 continents.

### [Quantium Retail Analytics – Potato Chip Sales Analysis](a year's worth potato chip transaction, and customer data for a region/README.md)

A retail analytics case study based on a year of potato chip transactions and customer data for a region.

Across three tasks it covers data cleaning, customer and store analysis, control-store selection, trial-store evaluation, and business reporting using SQL, Python, and Power BI.
